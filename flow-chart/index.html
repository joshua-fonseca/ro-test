The shared loops

  • Back to Search: the seller left, a Reconnect, and every --loop rejoin all end with the same walk (W, hold E) and a fresh search.
  • Close the news box: shared by the Reconnect path and the --loop rejoin.
  • Teleport: tried again on the card below after a full server.
  • Wait for the new server: Reconnect loops back into it.
  • Close the stall: every way through the stall (bought, dry run, gone, no cash) meets here.

Stops not drawn

  • Any button that does not show in time: Snipe stopped, take over.
  • 3 failed scans in a row: stop.
  • Roblox not in front: it pauses and sends nothing.
  • Ctrl+C: stop, and the Discord message is deleted.
  • Not enough cash with --loop: the stall is set up and the run stops.
